! Title: Bing Clean
! Description: Hides Copilot answers, AI mode, the MSN feed and other clutter on Bing, and blocks Bing telemetry, location reporting and tracking parameters. Leaves Microsoft Rewards point earning alone.
! Version: 1.0.1
! Expires: 7 days
! Homepage: https://github.com/zoltrons/bing-clean
! Last checked against live Bing: 2026-11-21
!
! Kept on purpose, because Rewards uses them to count points:
!   /rewardsapp/ (reportActivity, ncheader), /rewards/panelflyout, rewards.bing.com,
!   the Rewards badge, homepage quiz and info cards, and the FORM, form, PUBL, CREA, rnoreward and ocid parameters.

! === 1. Copilot and AI ===
! The "This summary was generated by AI" Copilot answer at the top of results.
! Bing puts entity info cards (people, films, places) in the same #copans_container,
! so match only answers that carry the AI label (.gs_aidisc) or AI chat text (.cht_root).
bing.com##li.b_ans:has(.gs_aidisc, #gs_infobbl, .cht_root)
! "AI MODE" and Copilot tabs in the scope bar
bing.com###b-scopeListItem-copilotsearch
bing.com###b-scopeListItem-conv
bing.com##.b_scopebar li:has(a[href*="/copilotsearch"], a[href*="/chat?"], a[href*="copilot.microsoft.com"])
! The Copilot chat container (#b_sydConvOuter, cib-serp) is NOT hidden:
! some Daily Set quizzes open inside it (filters=IsConversation).
! Copilot links and buttons anywhere on Bing
bing.com##a[href*="copilot.microsoft.com"]
! Copilot embedded on other sites. The /turing/ and /sydney/ backends stay open for the quizzes.
||copilot.microsoft.com^$third-party

! === 2. MSN and clutter ===
! MSN news and ad feed on the homepage
bing.com##.module.feed_bg
||assets.msn.com^$domain=bing.com
||api.msn.com^$domain=bing.com
||srtb.msn.com^$domain=bing.com
! MSN, Online Games, Microsoft 365 and "Get to Know Bing" in the homepage overflow menu
bing.com###msn_overflow
bing.com###games_overflow
bing.com###office_overflow
bing.com###cle_overflow
! "Advertise" in the hamburger menu and the floating Feedback button
bing.com###b_hb_sb_advertise
bing.com###sb_feedback_floating
! "People also ask"
bing.com###b_results > li.b_ans:has(.rqnaContainerwithfeedback)
! Search ads
bing.com##li.b_ad
bing.com##.b_adTop
bing.com##.b_adBottom
bing.com###b_pole:has(.b_ad)
! Uncomment to also hide the video carousel
!bing.com###b_topw > li.b_vidAns
! Visual search data usage pop-up
bing.com###sb_sbidialog

! === 3. Ads inside Rewards ===
! Checked on a signed-in panel and dashboard. Only cards that award no points.
! Rewards panel in the Bing header: Bing Wallpaper, "Redeem now" game promos, refer a friend
bing.com##.gbfo_c_bsic_variant:has(a[href*="/apps/wallpaper"])
bing.com##.gbfo_c_bsic_variant:has(a[href*="/redeem/sku/"])
bing.com##.flyout_control_halfUnit:has(a[href*="referandearn"])
! rewards.bing.com (dashboard and Earn pages): Bing Wallpaper / Bing app promo cards and the empty
! slide arrows left under them, "Refer and Earn" nav link
rewards.bing.com##a[href*="bing.com/apps/"]
rewards.bing.com##a[href*="bing.com/apps/"] + div:has([aria-label="Next slide"])
rewards.bing.com##a[href="/refer"], a[href*="/referandearn"]

! === 4. Privacy and telemetry ===
! Event and beacon logging (Rewards does not depend on these)
||bing.com/fd/ls/
||bing.com/web/xlsc.aspx
||bing.com/notifications/$xhr
||bing.com/feedback/$xhr
! Sends your exact latitude and longitude on every search
||bing.com/geolocation/write$xhr
! Microsoft ad and analytics networks
||bat.bing.com^
||c.bing.com^
||c.msn.com^
||browser.events.data.msn.com^
||clarity.ms^
||ads.msn.com^
||adnxs.com^$domain=bing.com|msn.com
! Autosuggest sends every keystroke to the server (this also turns off the suggestion dropdown)
||bing.com/AS/Suggestions$xhr
||bing.com/qsonhs.aspx$xhr

! === 5. Tracking parameters ===
! Bing search and vertical pages (FORM/form are kept for Rewards tasks)
||www.bing.com^$doc,removeparam=/^(cvid|qs|sp|lq|pq|sc|sk|ghsh|ghacc|ghpl|ghc|ajf|qpvt)$/
! Tracking codes Microsoft adds to outgoing links (all sites)
$removeparam=msclkid
$removeparam=/^WT\.mc_id$/
$removeparam=/^utm_/,domain=~rewards.bing.com|~microsoft.com
$removeparam=mkt_tok
$removeparam=/^(cmpid|icid|scid)$/,domain=microsoft.com|xbox.com|office.com|microsoft365.com|outlook.com|live.com

! === 6. Click tracking (/ck/a? redirect) ===
! uBO only runs this from a trusted source. A subscribed list is not trusted, so this line is ignored here.
! To use it, copy it into "My filters" and enable "Allow custom filters requiring trust" in uBO settings.
! It decodes u=a1<base64url> and goes straight to the result. Bing's own relative links decode to an invalid URL, so the rule leaves them alone.
||bing.com/ck/a?$urlskip=/[?&]u=a1([^&]+)/ -safebase64
